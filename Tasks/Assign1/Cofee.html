Cappuccino
An Italian classic with equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of milk foam. 
Ingredients: 1/3 espresso, 1/3 steamed milk, 1/3 milk foam.
Method:
Brew a shot of espresso.
Steam milk and create thick foam.
Pour the espresso into a cup.
Add the steamed milk, topping it with the thick foam
