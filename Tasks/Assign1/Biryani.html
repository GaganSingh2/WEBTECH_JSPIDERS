Biryani
A fragrant and flavorful rice dish cooked with aromatic spices and layered with vegetables. 
Recipe from Food Fusion 
Ingredients: Basmati rice, whole spices (cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, bay leaf), ginger-garlic paste, onions, tomatoes, coriander leaves, mint leaves, and biryani masala.
Method:
Soak and parboil the basmati rice.
Prepare the base gravy by sautéing aromatics and spices.
Layer the parboiled rice with the prepared gravy, fried onions, and fresh herbs.
Cook the biryani on dum (slow cooking) to infuse the flavors
