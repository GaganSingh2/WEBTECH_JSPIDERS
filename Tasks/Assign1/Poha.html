Ingredients
Serves 2 
1 ½ cups thick poha (flattened rice)
1 medium onion, finely chopped
1 medium potato, peeled and diced small (optional)
2–3 green chilies, slit or finely chopped
10–12 curry leaves
3–4 tablespoons peanuts
2 tablespoons cooking oil
½ teaspoon mustard seeds
½ teaspoon cumin seeds
¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
Salt, to taste
½–1 teaspoon sugar (optional, for balancing flavors)
Juice of ½ lemon
2 tablespoons fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped
A pinch of asafoetida (hing) (optional) 
Instructions
1. Prepare the poha
Place the poha in a colander or large-holed strainer. Rinse it gently under running water for about 30 seconds to wash and soften the flakes. Do not soak it, as this can make the poha mushy. Set the poha aside to drain. 
2. Fry the peanuts
In a pan or wok, heat 1 tablespoon of the oil over medium heat. Add the peanuts and fry, stirring often, until they are golden brown and crunchy. Remove the peanuts with a slotted spoon and set them aside. 
3. Temper the spices
In the same pan, heat the remaining oil. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter. Then, add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds. If using, add the asafoetida. 
4. Sauté the aromatics and vegetables
Add the curry leaves and green chilies, and sauté for 30 seconds. Add the chopped onions and cook until they turn translucent. If using potatoes, add them now with a sprinkle of salt. Cover the pan and cook for 3–4 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender. 
5. Add the seasoning
Add the turmeric powder and mix well. Cook for another minute to let the spices become fragrant. 
6. Combine the poha
Add the rinsed and drained poha to the pan. Sprinkle with salt and sugar (if using). Mix everything gently until the poha is coated evenly with the spices. Be careful not to break the flakes. 
7. Steam the poha
Cover the pan and let the poha steam on low heat for 2–3 minutes to become soft and hot. If the poha appears too dry, sprinkle a few drops of water over it. 
8. Finish and garnish
Turn off the heat. Stir in the roasted peanuts and freshly chopped coriander leaves. Squeeze the lemon juice over the poha and give it one final, gentle mix. 
9. Serve hot
Serve the poha hot, garnished with extra coriander leaves, sev (crunchy chickpea flour noodles), or grated coconut if desired
