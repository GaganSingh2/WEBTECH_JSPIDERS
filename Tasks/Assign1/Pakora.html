Ingredients
For the batter:
1 cup chickpea flour (besan)
½ tsp turmeric powder
½ tsp red chili powder
1 tsp cumin powder
1 tsp coriander powder
½ tsp garam masala
½ tsp carom seeds (ajwain), optional
Salt to taste
Water, as needed 
For the pakora:
2 cups mixed vegetables, finely chopped or sliced (options include onions, potatoes, spinach, or cauliflower)
1-2 green chilies, finely chopped
1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
A handful of fresh coriander leaves, chopped
Oil for deep frying 
Instructions
Prepare the batter: In a large bowl, whisk together the chickpea flour and all the spices (turmeric, red chili, cumin, coriander, garam masala, and carom seeds, if using) along with the salt.
Combine ingredients: Add the chopped vegetables, green chilies, ginger, and fresh coriander to the bowl. Mix everything together with your hands, squeezing the vegetables slightly to help them release their moisture.
Add water slowly: Gradually add a few tablespoons of water, mixing until the batter is just clinging to the vegetables. The mixture should be thick, not watery, as the vegetables will continue to release moisture.
Heat the oil: In a heavy-bottomed wok or frying pan, heat enough oil for deep frying over medium-high heat. You can test if the oil is ready by dropping a tiny amount of batter in; it should sizzle and rise immediately.
Fry the pakoras: Carefully drop spoonfuls of the pakora mixture into the hot oil. Avoid overcrowding the pan.
Cook until golden: Fry the pakoras for 2–3 minutes per side, or until they are golden brown and crispy.
Drain and serve: Remove the cooked pakoras with a slotted spoon and place them on a paper towel-lined plate to absorb excess oil. Serve hot with a side of mint or tamarind chutney.
