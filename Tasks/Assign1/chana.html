Ingredients: Chickpeas, onions, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, coriander leaves, and whole and ground spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric, and chili powder).
Method:
Soak chickpeas overnight and pressure cook until tender.
Sauté onions, ginger, and garlic until golden brown.
Add tomatoes and spices, cooking until the masala is fragrant.
Add the cooked chickpeas and simmer to let the flavors combine
