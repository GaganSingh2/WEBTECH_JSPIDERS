Paneer Tikka Masala
This restaurant-style recipe uses tender, marinated paneer (Indian cheese) that is grilled before being simmered in a creamy, spiced tomato gravy. 
Recipe from Hebbar's Kitchen 
Ingredients: Paneer, thick curd, ginger-garlic paste, capsicum, onion, tomato puree, and various spices (turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, cumin, and garam masala).
Method:
Marinate the paneer and vegetables in a spiced curd mixture for at least an hour.
Skewer the paneer and veggies and grill or pan-fry them until golden.
Prepare the gravy by sautéing aromatics and spices before adding tomato puree and cooking until the oil separates.
Add the grilled paneer to the gravy and simmer to finish
